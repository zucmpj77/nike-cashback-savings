## Where do real Nike promo codes come from?

Nike issues promo codes itself, on its own schedule, and applies them in your bag or at checkout — nowhere else. The company's help page says it plainly: codes are offered "occasionally" and are valid on Nike.com and in the Nike App. When a code works, you'll see the discount appear in your bag summary before you ever reach payment.

The mechanics are simple once you know where to look:

- **On Nike.com (in your bag):** in the "Summary" box, select "Do you have a Promo Code?", enter the code, hit Apply. The discount carries through to checkout if the code is valid for your items.
- **On Nike.com (at checkout):** in the "Payment" section, select "Do you have a gift card, product voucher, or promo code?" and apply it there.
- **In the Nike App:** the same options live under "Have a Promo Code?" in your bag, or under Payment → Add New Payment Method → Add Promo Code at checkout.

The coupon sites ranking for this search will hand you all kinds of high-percentage codes with confident discounts attached. Treat those as leads, not facts. Nike's own FAQ only confirms the codes it publishes, and a code that's entered correctly but still refuses to apply usually means one thing, per Nike: "items in your bag may be excluded from the offer." New releases and some popular colorways get excluded from promotions a lot.
